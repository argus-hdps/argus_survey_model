Argus Array Simulator
=====================

ArgusSim simulates the Argus Array and its survey. It models the array's telescope placement and sky coverage, the
system throughput of the survey bands, the sky background, the delivered PSF with seeing, and the filter pattern
across the array. From these it computes survey statistics: how often each part of the sky is observed and in which
band, and the limiting magnitude of single exposures and stacks under simulated weather, seeing and Moon.
